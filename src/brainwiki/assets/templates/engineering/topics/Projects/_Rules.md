# Projects

- One folder per project, named after it (`Projects/Sync Rewrite/`), with an
  `_Index.md` that says the goal, the status, and where the code and the
  tracking live.
- Inside a project: `type: decision` notes for choices and their reasons,
  and dated accounts of what happened (incidents, launches, benchmark runs)
  in `records/` (`records/2026-10-16 Sync Outage.md`).
- A finished or dropped project keeps its folder; set `bw-stage: done` or
  `bw-stage: dropped` on its `_Index.md`.
