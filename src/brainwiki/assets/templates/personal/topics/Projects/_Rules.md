# Projects

- One folder per project, named after it (`Projects/Kitchen Renovation/`),
  with an `_Index.md` that says what the project is, its goal, and its
  status.
- Inside a project: decisions as `type: decision` notes, and dated accounts
  of what happened in `records/` (`records/2026-10-16 Contractor Visit.md`).
- A finished or dropped project keeps its folder; set `bw-stage: done` or
  `bw-stage: dropped` on its `_Index.md`.
- Knowledge that outlives the project goes to Reference, linked from the
  project.
