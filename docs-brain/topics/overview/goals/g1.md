# G1: Files are the source of truth

**Files are the source of truth.** Everything the system knows is in the brain directory, readable and editable by humans with any editor, and works in Obsidian.

**How we will know (v0.1):** Deleting the index and rebuilding it loses nothing. A brain round-trips through Obsidian edits without lint errors.
