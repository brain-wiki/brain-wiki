# D3: Frontmatter is flat (scalars and lists of scalars)

Frontmatter is flat (scalars and lists of scalars). Claims and relations are frontmatter lists of strings (`bw-claims`, `bw-<verb>`), so the body stays prose and Obsidian shows them as properties with live links. Format 1 kept them in `## Claims` and `## Relations` body sections.

**Alternatives:** Nested YAML objects (Obsidian cannot show or edit them); body sections (format 1: data mixed into prose, invisible to the Properties view)
