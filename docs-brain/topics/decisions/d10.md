# D10: No access control inside a brain for now

No access control inside a brain for now: every host that may use a brain reads all of it. Separate brains and host brain limits are the boundary; privacy inside a brain is being redesigned around who receives content.

**Alternatives:** Ordered levels per note and topic with a maximum per profile, plus topic allowlists (shipped, then removed: a level did not say what it protects against or from whom); unordered label sets; embedding-based classification
