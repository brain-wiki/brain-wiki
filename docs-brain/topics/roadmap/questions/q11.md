# Q11: How do facts about the outside world stay current? Versions, APIs,…

How do facts about the outside world stay current? Versions, APIs, prices, and people's roles go stale without anyone noticing.

**Options:** (a) Rely on supersession when someone notices. (b) A review date and source URL per fact, flagged by lint when past due, and a pass that re-checks them against their sources. (c) Ranking that decays with age.

**Status and recommendation:** Open. Option (b) was designed around per-claim metadata, which format 3 removed. Notes can cite URLs in `bw-sources`, but no note-level review date or lint check is designed.
