# Q11: How do world facts stay current? Agent knowledge about the outside…

**How do world facts stay current?** Agent knowledge about the outside world (versions, APIs, prices, people's roles) goes stale silently.

**Options:** (a) Rely on supersession when someone notices. (b) Activate the reserved `valid_until` and a per-claim `source` URL; lint and the health view flag claims past due or old and unverified; a verify pass re-checks them against their sources. (c) Time-decay ranking.

**Recommendation:** **Open (format 3):** (b) relied on claim metadata, which is gone. Note-level `bw-sources` URLs exist; a note-level review date and a lint check are not designed.
