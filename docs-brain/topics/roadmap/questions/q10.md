# Q10: Should retrieval weigh who said it?

**Should retrieval weigh who said it?**

**Options:** (a) No: rank by relevance only, as today. (b) Label each result and orientation item with its origin (yours vs learned by agents) without changing rank. (c) Also rank owner-origin above agent-origin on ties, per profile.

**Recommendation:** **Moot until Q9 is settled:** format 3 records no origin per claim or note (D33), so there is nothing to label or rank by. Git history and `bw-sources` are the only record of who wrote what.
