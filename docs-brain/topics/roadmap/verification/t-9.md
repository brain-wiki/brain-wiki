# T-9: What hook timeouts and output limits do hosts enforce?

What hook timeouts and output limits do hosts enforce?

**Result or test:** **Antigravity:** a hook past its timeout is killed and its output dropped, and the model call proceeds. Hooks run one after another before every model call. An `ephemeralMessage` is cut at about 49 KB. Other hosts not tested; brain-wiki ships no hooks now.
