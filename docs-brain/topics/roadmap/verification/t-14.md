# T-14: Does Obsidian treat links in list properties (bw-depends_on

Does Obsidian treat links in list properties (`bw-depends_on: ["[[Note]] — why"]`, with text after the link) as links: in backlinks and the graph, and rewritten on rename?

**Test:** **Narrowed by format 3:** relations with text after the link moved to the body. Only `bw-superseded_by` and `bw-sources` hold links in properties, as bare `"[[note]]"` values. Open a format 3 brain in a scratch vault; rename a note one of them names and check the property, the backlinks pane, and the graph.
