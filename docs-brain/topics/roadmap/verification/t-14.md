# T-14: Does Obsidian treat links in list properties as links, in backlinks and…

Does Obsidian treat links in list properties as links, in backlinks and the graph, and rewrite them on rename?

**Result or test:** Open, and narrower in format 3: only `bw-superseded_by` and `bw-sources` hold links in properties, as bare `"[[note]]"` values. Open a format 3 brain as a scratch vault, rename a note one of them names, and check the property, the backlinks pane, and the graph.
